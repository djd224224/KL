Overall Summary

June 29 produced zero trades and zero P&L — not from a quiet market, but from a broken order pipeline. The forecasting engine ran cleanly: full snapshots landed for all 20 cities, correctly calling a widespread late-June heat event (Phoenix 103.5°F, Dallas 98.5°F, San Antonio/OKC/Atlanta in the mid-to-high 90s). The bot then tried to act on those signals and attempted 369 orders across 32 markets in 17 cities between 5:04 and 7:07 AM ET — every single one rejected with HttpError(410 Gone). This is the same legacy-endpoint retirement that has kept the bot dark since June 18: the last fills in KXHIGH_fills_enriched are dated June 18, and no orders were recorded for any day June 19–29. The model wanted to be in this heat wave and could not place a single contract. The good news: the fix has landed. The V2 order-endpoint migration (commit 4789e47) deployed overnight, and as of today (June 30) the bot has logged 975 fresh orders with zero 410 errors — meaning June 29 was the final dark day of an 11-day execution outage. The story of the day is not a P&L number; it is the cost of those 11 missed trading days, now ended.
