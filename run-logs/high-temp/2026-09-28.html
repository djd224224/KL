The third slot matches the bot's own 9/29 WU reads (Seattle 62°F, San Francisco 74°F, Phoenix 77°F), so the 9/28 value sits in slot two: LA's 77°F, Las Vegas's 82°F and Seattle's 66°F are exactly what the 7 AM run used.
