How the numbers are built

Modeled rewards integrate the bot's own reward estimate (est_frac × pool $/day) over every gap between full cycles, capped at 15 minutes, from the cycle logs. It is a model and sits before Kalshi's $1-per-market-per-period floor; credits land 1–2 days after a period ends and show up in the history as credited.

Trading P&L is mark-to-market on the bot's own book: realized in the window (closing fills and settlements) plus the change in open positions valued at mid (the last trade when a book is empty), from the 5-minute position snapshots. Exits the bot does not book itself are valued at Kalshi's actual settlement: scalar settlements (sports ladders and escalators, which the bot logs as "manual offsets") and positions that leave its book with no record. Only an exit Kalshi has not settled counts as a transfer at its last mark. Audited 9/29 against a replay of Kalshi's own fills and settlement results: equal to the cent for 9/16, 9/22, 9/27, 9/28, 9/29 and the 7-day window.

Net is the two added together. Days are ET calendar days; the bot's own halt counter rolls at 5am CT and is shown under Risk.
