How the numbers are built

Modeled rewards integrate the bot's own reward estimate (est_frac × pool $/day) over every gap between full cycles, capped at 15 minutes, from the cycle logs. It is a model and sits before Kalshi's $1-per-market-per-period floor; credits land 1–2 days after a period ends and show up in the history as credited.

Trading P&L is mark-to-market on the bot's own book: realized in the window (closing fills and settlements) plus the change in open positions valued at mid (the last trade when a book is empty), from the 5-minute position snapshots. Exits the bot does not book itself are valued at Kalshi's actual settlement: scalar settlements (sports ladders and escalators, which the bot logs as "manual offsets") and positions that leave its book with no record. Only an exit Kalshi has not settled counts as a transfer at its last mark. Audited 9/29 against a replay of Kalshi's own fills and settlement results: equal to the cent for 9/16, 9/22, 9/27, 9/28, 9/29 and the 7-day window.

Net is the two added together. Days are ET calendar days; the bot's own halt counter rolls at 5am CT and is shown under Risk. Kalshi fees are not deducted; the IMM digest's fee total runs $0–0.60 a day.

P&L explain (Drivers). Trading P&L splits exactly into three parts. Carry: marks is the position held at the window's start, re-marked from its start mark to its end mark (or to its last mark before it left the book). Carry: settled is that start position in a market Kalshi settled during the window, from its start mark to the settlement. New fills is the rest: the window's own fills, marked to the window's end or to the settlement.

Mark-outs cover maker fills only (no pads, no taker fills). They are per contract and signed for our side. At fill is the edge: the fill price against the mid of the external book the bot read the cycle before the fill (none on a book 50¢+ wide). 5m, 30m and 4h use the first 5-minute position snapshot at least that long after the fill; a mark must land within 5 minutes for 5m and within 15 minutes otherwise, so a fill that closed the position has none. To date uses Kalshi's settlement, else the bot's mark while it holds the market, else the external mid it quotes against, else Kalshi's touch under the bot's mark rule. Coverage is rewards ÷ what the new fills lost: under 1× the pool does not pay for the flow.

Exp. net/day is a model: the rewards the bot's estimator gives the quotes resting now, plus the family's average trading P&L per day over the last 7 days. Typical day is the family's mean net per complete ET day over the 7 and 30 days before the window. The σ mark flags a window 1.5+ standard deviations from that mean, pro-rated to the window's length.

Worst case is the loss from today's marks to settlement if everything goes against the book. It is netted inside an event: across its strikes when every market prices the same number (greater / less / between), and across names when Kalshi marks the event mutually exclusive. Mention words and anything whose structure has not been read count in full. Summed across events, it assumes every event goes wrong at once. On a past day it is the book at that day's close.

Paid rate is what Kalshi credited per dollar of modeled reward on settled events (imm_reward_recon's calibration). The raw basis covers the $1 per-market-per-period floor and the model error together. The older floored basis covers the model error only. Account credits include other bots quoting the same markets, which can push a family above 1×.
